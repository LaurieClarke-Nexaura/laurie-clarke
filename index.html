How I see the work

The problem is often in the interaction.

Most people look for performance problems in the strategy, structure, technology, individual or team. The real breakdown is often in the interaction between them. I work across those boundaries to find the constraint that is actually limiting performance and determine what needs to be solved first.

See the whole system

Understand how strategy, structure, technology, customer needs, people and capacity are interacting rather than treating each issue in isolation.

Find the real constraint

Separate symptoms from the constraint creating them so time, money and leadership attention are not spent solving the wrong problem.

Fix what matters first

Remove the friction or redesign what needs to change, then stay close enough to the work to help the solution produce results.

The form of engagement follows the problem. Sometimes the answer is a decision or focused redesign; sometimes it requires hands-on leadership or a broader transformation. The value is knowing where to intervene first — and then making the change real.

About Laurie

A strategist’s lens. An operator’s instinct.

Laurie Clarke is an executive advisor, operator, complex problem solver and transformation leader with more than two decades of experience helping organizations grow, change and solve difficult problems.

Today, Laurie works directly with CEOs, founders, executives and leadership teams on complex operating and growth challenges. Her work ranges from executive advisory and complex problem solving to fractional leadership, customer-experience and operating redesign, enterprise transformation, speaking and facilitation.

Earlier in her career, Laurie was COO and co-owner of The Tatham Group, where she led large-scale transformations across North America, Europe and Asia. Across her career, she has worked with Fortune 500 executives, founders, boards and leadership teams on strategy, customer experience, operating-model redesign, organizational change and complex implementation.

Her work is deliberately cross-industry. The problems she is brought in to solve — how strategy becomes execution, where capacity is being lost, what is constraining growth, and why customer, operating or people issues are reinforcing one another — sit beneath sector mechanics. Working across industries gives her a wider pattern library and enough distance to challenge assumptions that can become invisible from inside the business, while the people closest to the work bring the technical depth.

What clients tend to value is her ability to move between the strategic and the practical: to see connections across customer, operations, people and leadership, find the constraint beneath the symptoms and stay engaged long enough to make the change real.

Laurie holds a Bachelor of Commerce with distinction from the University of Toronto, with studies spanning commerce and finance, human biology and economics.
